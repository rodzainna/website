The customer-facing application for Luxsurance, giving policyholders a way to manage their insurance directly, alongside the admin-facing dashboard.

## What I worked on

- Built UI components and templates with **Vue.js and Tailwind CSS** for customer-facing features.
- Integrated REST API endpoints for customer account and policy data.
- Worked within the same Agile/Scrum delivery process as the admin dashboard team.
