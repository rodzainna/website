An embeddable booking widget that lets a veterinary practice's clients schedule appointments directly from the practice's own website, backed by the same GraphQL API as the rest of the platform.

## What I worked on

- Built and maintained UI for the booking flow using **React, Vite, and TypeScript**, on top of the shared **shadcn/ui + Tailwind CSS** design system.
- Focused on accessibility and responsiveness, since the widget has to work well embedded in a wide variety of third-party sites.
- Wired the flow up to the platform's **GraphQL API** for availability, scheduling, and confirmation.
