A time-tracking product with a Slack-native workflow — clock in/out and check-ins happen via Slack slash commands and interactive messages, backed by a Vue.js reporting dashboard and Laravel API.

## What I worked on

- Took ownership of the product following the original developer's departure, stabilizing a backlog-heavy application.
- Built Slack slash-command and interactive-message workflows, including automated check-in prompts and timeout-based sign-out handling.
- Developed the **Vue.js + Tailwind CSS** reporting dashboard.
- Contributed to the **PHP/Laravel** backend and the **Stripe** billing integration.
