Bilingual **WordPress Multisite** platforms I helped build for UAE government clients, including the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MNFCA) and the Ministry of Culture and Knowledge Development (MCKD), plus MCKD's whole network of subsites.

## What I worked on

- Built and supported multilingual **WordPress Multisite** platforms, English and Arabic, including right-to-left (RTL) and left-to-right (LTR) layouts.
- Delivered custom PHP themes across MCKD's subsites so everything stayed consistent.
- Handled on-page SEO basics — semantic markup, metadata, performance — since these were public government sites.
