Ongoing theme maintenance on two **Shopify** stores: Joanna Czech, a luxury skincare and beauty studio brand, and Dr. Flora Kim, a dermatology practice with a curated skincare shop.

## What I worked on

- Configured theme settings, sections, collections and page content in the Shopify admin.
- Edited **Liquid** templates, sections and snippets for layout and content changes.
- Fixed styling and responsive layout issues with **Tailwind CSS**.
- Fixed storefront bugs and kept each store's Shopify apps configured and up to date.
